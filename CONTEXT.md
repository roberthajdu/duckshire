# Duckshire

A duck-themed idle RPG in the Melvor Idle style: one adventurer duck performs one activity at a time, in real time and while away, and progresses through skills and gear.

## Language

**Duck**:
The single adventurer the player controls.
_Avoid_: Character, hero, avatar, champion

**Breed**:
The cosmetic species of the Duck (for example Mallard or Pekin), chosen at creation with no effect on Power.
_Avoid_: Class, race, species

**Skill**:
A trainable ability that levels up through repeated use of its Actions.
_Avoid_: Profession, stat

**Action**:
The one activity the Duck is currently performing, which ticks repeatedly and yields resources and Skill experience.
_Avoid_: Task, job, activity

**Region**:
A themed area of the world with its own Skills' Actions, resources and signature boss, unlocked by reaching a level gate.
_Avoid_: Zone, map, biome, level

**Pondhaven**:
The first Region, with a pond setting.

**Power**:
The Duck's overall strength, which the player continually grows through gear and Skill levels.
_Avoid_: Level (reserved for Skills), rank

**Material Tier**:
A step on a Skill-gated ladder of crafted gear materials, each tier requiring a higher Skill level.
_Avoid_: Gear level, grade

**Mastery**:
A per-Action level that grows with repetition and grants bonuses for that specific Action.
_Avoid_: Proficiency, Action level

**Skillcape**:
A wearable item bought when a Skill reaches its maximum level, granting a permanent perk for that Skill.
_Avoid_: Cape, trophy, badge

**Feathers**:
The single currency, earned by selling items and spent on purchases.
_Avoid_: Gold, coins, GP

**Cycle**:
One fixed-length repetition of an Action, after which its yield and Skill experience are granted.
_Avoid_: Tick, turn, loop

**Expansion**:
A paid content pack that adds Regions, Skills or Actions to the game.
_Avoid_: DLC, season, update

**Offline Progress**:
Progress the Duck earns by continuing the current Action while the player is away.
_Avoid_: AFK gains, idle gains
